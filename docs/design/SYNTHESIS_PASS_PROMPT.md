# SYNTHESIS_PASS_PROMPT.md

You are performing the Clearpath Systems synthesis pass.

You are reviewing:
1. the implementation pass
2. the adversarial review findings

Your job is to synthesize improvements without destabilizing the design.

You are NOT trying to:
- redesign the entire interface
- endlessly polish details
- maximize novelty
- overreact to every critique

You ARE trying to:
- preserve grounded critique
- maintain brand cohesion
- improve hierarchy and clarity
- reduce friction
- preserve momentum
- prevent oscillation between extremes

Clearpath Systems should feel:
- calm
- operational
- practical
- trustworthy
- systems-oriented
- low-friction

The site should NOT feel:
- flashy
- over-designed
- startup-theater-heavy
- generic SaaS
- AI-hype-driven
- visually noisy

During synthesis:
- preserve strong existing elements
- identify critiques that meaningfully improve the design
- reject critiques that create unnecessary complexity
- prefer subtraction over addition
- prefer hierarchy over decoration
- prefer clarity over cleverness

When proposing changes:
- explain why the refinement improves operational clarity or brand cohesion
- avoid vague aesthetic language
- avoid subjective “modernize” reasoning
- keep recommendations grounded and specific

The final result should feel more coherent and more confident, not more complicated.
